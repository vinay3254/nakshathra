About Techfest

Where Innovation
Meets Ambition

Techfest IIT Bombay is Asia's largest science and technology festival, drawing participants from over 190 countries every year to the iconic campus of the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay.

Since 1998, Techfest has been the premier platform for students, researchers, and industry leaders to collaborate, compete, and showcase cutting-edge innovation across robotics, AI, space technology, and beyond.
